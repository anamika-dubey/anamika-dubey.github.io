Anamika Dubey delivered the 2026 Anjan Bose Lecture, “Engineering Resilient Power Grid: From Understanding Risk to Informing Decisions,” at WSU in Pullman. The lecture explored how outage data, vulnerability models, and optimization can inform decisions about extreme-weather risk and power-grid resilience, including emerging challenges in the Pacific Northwest.
