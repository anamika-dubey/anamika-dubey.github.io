Anamika Dubey joined a multidisciplinary WSU team awarded a $1 million NSF grant to develop community-centered planning tools for wildfire-related power outages. Led by Ji Yun Lee, the project combines community engagement, wildfire simulation, and power-grid resilience planning. [WSU story](https://news.wsu.edu/news/2025/04/29/researchers-receive-nsf-grant-to-help-communities-better-plan-for-wildfires-and-power-outages/).
