Sajjad Uddin Mahmud and Anamika Dubey shared a [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04368) presenting a three-stage Bayesian framework for estimating how likely gas-fired generators are to lose capacity during extreme winter weather, how much capacity remains available, and how long those events last.
