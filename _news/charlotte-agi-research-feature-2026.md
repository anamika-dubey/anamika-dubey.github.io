Charlotte Wertz was featured by the Advanced Grid Institute for her joint WSU–PNNL research on extreme-weather impacts and power-grid resilience, advised by Anamika Dubey and PNNL’s Jason Fuller. The feature highlights her work on quantifying weather-related risks and making resilience analysis useful for utilities and communities.
