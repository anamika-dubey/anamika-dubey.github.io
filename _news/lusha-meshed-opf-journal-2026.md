Lusha Wang and Anamika Dubey, with a collaborator, published [Linearized Optimal Power Flow for Multi-Phase Meshed Unbalanced Power Distribution Systems]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}#wang2026linearizedoptimalpower) in IEEE Transactions on Smart Grid. The work develops linearized power-flow and optimization models for unbalanced distribution systems with meshed networks and diverse transformer configurations.
