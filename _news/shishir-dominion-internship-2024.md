Shishir Lamichhane began a summer internship with the Transmission Planning group at Dominion Energy in Richmond, VA. He completed the internship in August 2024, developing a GUI-based automation tool for transfer-capability planning. He subsequently presented his internship work at the CIGRE Grid of the Future Symposium in Raleigh, North Carolina, in November 2024.
