Rabayet Sadnan and Anamika Dubey, with colleagues, taught “Advances in Distribution Optimal Power Flow (DOPF): Theory, Implementation, and Applications” at the IEEE PES General Meeting in Montréal, Canada. The tutorial included hands-on distribution-system optimization with open-source tools.
