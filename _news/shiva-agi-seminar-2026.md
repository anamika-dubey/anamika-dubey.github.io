Shiva Poudel was invited to present “Increasing Grid Utilization Through Stochastic Operating Envelopes and Asset-Aware Controls” at the WSU–PNNL Advanced Grid Institute seminar on September 30, 2026. The announced talk focused on using stochastic operating envelopes, asset-aware optimal power flow, and large-scale simulation to unlock capacity from existing distribution grids.
