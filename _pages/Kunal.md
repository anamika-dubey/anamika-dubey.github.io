### Kunal Shankar

**PhD candidate, Spring 2024**

I am a PhD candidate in the School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at Washington State University, Pullman. I received my B.Tech. in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Birla Institute of Technology Mesra, India, in 2023. Before joining WSU, I worked as a Deputy Manager and Energy Analyst at Schneider Electric.

My research centers on system identification and estimation, with a particular focus on scientific grey-box modeling of buildings. Building on this work, I am developing smart-grid modeling and demand-response approaches that use building-level flexibility to support grid reliability, capacity, and decarbonization. I am a member of the Energy Systems Innovation Center (ESIC) and am advised by Dr. Anamika Dubey.

Outside of research, I enjoy working out and playing ping pong.
