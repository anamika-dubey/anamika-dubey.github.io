### Daniel Glover

**PhD alumnus, Fall 2021 to Spring 2026**

Daniel Glover (S’19-M’26) received a Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Washington State University in May 2026, with a focus on power systems engineering and computational science and was recognized as an Outstanding Research Assistant. He earned a B.S. in Electrical Engineering and an M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Oklahoma and spent four years as a doctoral engineering intern with the Power System Modeling group at Pacific Northwest National Laboratory. He is currently a Lead Engineer in Advanced Computational Methods for Power Systems within the Energy Systems group at Eaton Research Labs. His research focuses on high-performance computing for grid security and resiliency, as well as safety-critical AI and machine learning for constrained power-system optimization, with an emphasis on robust learning-based methods for utility-scale deployment.
