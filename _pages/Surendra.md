### Surendra Bajagain

**PhD alumnus, Spring 2019 to Fall 2023**

Surendra Bajagain (Member, IEEE) received his MS from the Department of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at South Dakota State University, Brookings, South Dakota, in 2017, and his PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Washington State University, Pullman, Washington, in 2023. His research interests include distribution system state estimation and distribution system modeling and planning.

**Thesis:** Enhancing Distribution System Situational Awareness Using Smart Meters
