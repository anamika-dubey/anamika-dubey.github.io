**Chandra Kant Jat** - As a Postdoctoral Research Associate, I work on multi-period energy storage optimization and reinforcement learning for power system operation and control. I earned my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from KU Leuven, Belgium, in 2024, following an M.Tech. in Power Systems from IIT Bombay and a B.Tech. from IIT Roorkee. My doctoral work focused on modeling and optimization of hybrid AC/DC power systems, developing power flow and optimal power flow models for the unbalanced operation of bipolar HVDC grids. I have contributed to multiple European projects -PROMOTioN, NEPTUNE, and FLEXPLAN- and spent time as a Visiting Scholar at Los Alamos National Laboratory in 2023.
