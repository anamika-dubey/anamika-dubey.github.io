### Ninad Kiran Gaikwad

**PhD candidate, Spring 2022**

I am originally from Mumbai, Maharashtra, India. I received my BTech in Electrical Engineering from Veermata Jijabai Technological Institute (VJTI), Mumbai, in 2012, my MTech in Power Electronics and Power Systems from Sardar Patel College of Engineering, Mumbai, in 2016, my MS in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Florida, Gainesville, in 2021, and my MS in Computer Science from Washington State University (WSU) in 2026. I am currently a PhD candidate in Electrical Engineering at WSU, conducting research in the SCALE Lab, and also work as a Lead Power Systems Developer at iGridSim. My research focuses on physics-informed and data-driven modeling, reduced-order thermal modeling, Model Predictive Control (MPC), Reinforcement Learning (RL), co-simulation, and intelligent control of grid-interactive buildings and energy systems. My broader interests lie at the intersection of power systems, control, optimization, and artificial intelligence. In the future, I am interested in joining academia as a faculty member and developing an independent research program in intelligent and autonomous energy systems. Outside of research, I enjoy long road trips, playing table tennis, and playing the guitar.
