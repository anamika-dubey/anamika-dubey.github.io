Aryan Ritwajeet Jha (PhD candidate, Fall 2022) - My research focuses on computationally efficient multi-period optimization of power distribution systems, particularly with energy storage. Internships across an electric reliability organization, corporate R&D, and an original equipment manufacturer have reinforced my interest in combining an industry career with continued research. I enjoy the journey from practical engineering needs to modeling requirements, understanding their computational complexity, and developing algorithms that make these problems tractable -- ultimately delivering useful tools for power system engineers.

I received my BE in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus, in 2020 and pursued graduate work in Power System Operation and Control at IIT Delhi from 2020 to 2022.

My appreciation for my friends, colleagues, and professors has only grown throughout this journey. Cultivating that environment is part of doing good research -- through kindness, shared curiosity, and helping one another grow. Outside research, I enjoy badminton, films, reading, and visiting art museums. If you are considering SCALE Lab, please feel free to reach out -- I would be happy to share my experience of our research and lab culture.
