### Rahul Jha

**PhD alumnus, Fall 2016 to Fall 2020**

Rahul Jha is a Senior Engineer at ComEd. He began his Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering at Washington State University in Fall 2016 and graduated in Fall 2020. His expertise spans distribution system optimization, DER integration, inverter-based resources, and real-time power system simulation. He has led and contributed to utility and industry initiatives focused on advancing grid reliability, resilience, and operational efficiency.

**Thesis:** Network-Level Optimization for Volt/Var Control in Unbalanced Electric Power Distribution Systems
