### Shiva Poudel

**PhD alumnus, Fall 2016 to Summer 2020**

Shiva's research covers distribution system modeling and analysis, restoration, resilience assessment, and software applications for modern power distribution systems. He received his Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from Washington State University, his M.S. in Electrical Engineering from South Dakota State University, and his B.E. in Electrical Engineering from the Institute of Engineering. He joined Pacific Northwest National Laboratory in 2020 after summer internships at Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories in 2018 and EPRI in 2019.

**Thesis:** Quantifying and Improving Resilience in Power Distribution Systems
