### Subho Paul

**Former postdoctoral researcher**

**Career:** Assistant Professor at IIT BHU.

Subho worked as a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at Washington State University, Pullman. He received his M.E. in Electrical Engineering with a power systems specialization from Jadavpur University, Kolkata, India, in 2016, and his PhD in Electrical Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee, India, in 2021. From October 2021 to September 2022, he worked as a Postdoctoral Research Associate under the Indo-US UI-ASSIST project at IIT Roorkee. His research interests include power system optimization, distribution network optimal power flow, microgrid energy management, and applications of deep learning in power systems. His work at WSU focused on the impact of communication and data issues on distribution network OPF analysis. He received the University Gold Medal for standing first in the M.E. program at Jadavpur University in 2016.
