**Aryan Ritwajeet Jha (PhD candidate, Fall 2022)** - I am a PhD candidate in Electrical Engineering at Washington State University, advised by Dr. Anamika Dubey. My research focuses on making multi-period optimization of power distribution systems more computationally efficient, particularly when energy storage couples decisions across time. I enjoy translating mathematical ideas into practical solutions through programming, modeling, and scientific computing, with the goal of giving power system engineers accessible tools for complex analyses.

My academic background includes a BE in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus, in 2020 and graduate work in Power System Operation and Control at IIT Delhi from 2020 to 2022. My internships, including my time with Tesla Energy's Power Systems team, have strengthened my interest in an industry career that combines engineering practice with continued research.

The people I work and learn with are a central part of what makes this journey meaningful to me. My appreciation for my friends, colleagues, and professors has only grown over the years, and I enjoy collaborating with people of diverse backgrounds and skill sets.
