### Rabayet Sadnan

**PhD alumnus, Spring 2023**

Rabayet's research focuses on scalable optimization, distributed control and coordination, and power system modeling and analysis. He earned his B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology in 2015 and 2017, followed by an M.Sc. in Mathematics and a Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Washington State University in 2021 and 2023.

**Thesis:** Distributed Computation and Optimization for Electric Power Distribution Systems
