# WSU Power publication venue filter

Source: https://school.eecs.wsu.edu/academics/graduate-program/phd-student-publication-requirement-list/#power

Power section supplied by the user and reviewed October 6, 2026: 20 journals and 11 conferences. `_data/publication_venues.yml` records membership independently of Popular/Other grouping. Canonical journal titles expand the abbreviated names on the WSU list.

The filter identifies venues on this list, including historical papers. It does not establish whether a paper or student satisfies acceptance, first-author, journal-count, or exam requirements. Unknown venues are not classified as approved. IEEE Access, PMAPS, TPEC, and IET Smart Grid are not on the supplied Power list; popularity alone does not confer approval.
